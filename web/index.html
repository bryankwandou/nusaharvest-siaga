Cash arrives before the harvest fails.

Why this exists

Crop insurance does not reach most small farmers.

Classic insurance asks a farmer to pay up front, prove a loss, and wait months for an adjuster. By the time money comes, the planting window is gone.

How it works

Four steps, no adjuster.

Sponsor funds a campaign

A company, local government or donor locks USDC for one district and one season.

Farmers register once

Sign-up runs over WhatsApp. The roster is sealed as a Merkle root before the season starts.

Rain decides

Satellite-based rainfall is compared with the district's floor. No one inspects a field.

Money moves

If rain is short, each farmer receives the same amount. If not, the sponsor gets the funds back.
