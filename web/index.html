Siaga Tanam · a sponsor-funded conditional grant

Cash arrives before the harvest fails.

Sponsors lock their own funds before the season under one public rule: if rainfall falls below the district floor, the grant is paid. Farmers pay nothing, ever, and receive rupiah in their e-wallet.

Why this exists

Planting money arrives too late.

When the October rain fails, seed and labour are already spent. Help that depends on assessing a loss after harvest comes weeks or months later, and the fastest cash is a trader's advance repaid with a cheap harvest.

How it works

Four steps, one public rule.

Sponsor funds a campaign

A CSR team, zakat institution or local government locks its own funds in an escrow on Solana for one district and one season.

Farmers register once

Sign-up runs over WhatsApp. The roster is sealed as a Merkle root before the season starts.

Rain decides

Satellite-based rainfall is compared with the district's floor. No one inspects a field.

Grant is paid

If rain is short, each farmer receives the same amount in rupiah, sent to their e-wallet by a licensed payment provider. If not, the funds go back to the sponsor.

Alongside AUTP

A fast top-up, not a replacement.

The government's AUTP scheme protects against verified harvest loss. Siaga Tanam does not replace it. It adds a small grant that arrives during the planting window, when cash matters most.

TopicAUTP (government crop insurance)Siaga Tanam
