# NusaHarvest brand

Product: NusaHarvest Siaga Tanam, rainfall-triggered planting support for Indonesian farmers on Solana.

## Mark
A raindrop above a young rice shoot, sitting in a rounded square with a field line along the bottom. Rain decides, the crop is what it protects.

- `logo-mark.svg` (64 grid): full detail, 32px and up.
- `favicon.svg` (32 grid): drops the field band, thicker stem, for 16 to 32px.
- `logo.svg`: mark plus wordmark. "Nusa" in forest, "Harvest" in harvest gold. Wordmark uses Fraunces; outline the text before use in print or off-web tools.
- Clear space: one quarter of the mark height on all sides. Do not recolour the drop, rotate, or add effects.

## Type
- Display: Fraunces 500/650, tight tracking (-0.02em).
- Body and UI: Inter 400/500/600, 16px base, 1.6 line height.
- Numbers use tabular figures in data views.

## Voice
- Plain, specific, calm. Say what happens and when: "If rain is short, each farmer gets the same amount."
- Show the number and its source. Label modelled data as modelled and examples as examples.
- Say what is not built yet. Never show fake balances or fake transactions.
- Bilingual: English default, Bahasa Indonesia written naturally, not word for word.
- Avoid: emoji, hype ("revolutionize", "seamless", "empower", "unlock", "game-changing"), and crypto jargon where a farmer-facing word exists.
